# Logos

The F1 and team logos are trademarks of their owners, so they are not kept in the repository.
Put your own copies here (transparent background) and the app picks them up:

- `f1.svg` (or `f1.png`): header of the session picker
- `<team>.png`: team logo in the replay leaderboard, one of
  `red-bull`, `racing-bulls`, `ferrari`, `mercedes`, `mclaren`, `aston-martin`, `alpine`,
  `renault`, `williams`, `audi`, `sauber`, `haas`, `cadillac`
